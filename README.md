Welcome to Nyan Runner, a fast-paced tapping game where quick reflexes and sharp focus are the only way to survive. Tap the stars, dodge chaos, and score your way into the Legendary Star Hunter hall of fame!

---

## 🎮 Gameplay Overview

- Tap the Stars before they vanish. Miss one? Say goodbye to a perfect score.
- Avoid tapping the background – every wrong tap cuts your score in HALF!
- Watch out for the Random UFO 👽 – it appears when you least expect it.
- No Silent Mode Allowed – Play LOUD or don't play at all. 🔊
- You get 3 lives. Lose them all, and it's game over (Nyaying Heart Attack included).
- Score 6389 to unlock the title of Legendary Star Hunter.
